## What has been falsified

**"It is the GPU."** The camera crashes identically on an RTX 4090 with the exact driver
`RESUME.md` specifies. C-23's own VRAM measurement (peak 3776 MiB of 16376) already ruled
memory out. `RESUME.md` has been corrected and its "verified on a 4090" claim marked
UNCONFIRMED — nobody has evidence of this camera working on any machine.

**"It is the ROS 2 image writer specifically."** A capture route with **no OmniGraph
writer anywhere in it** — annotators pulled with `get_data()`, published from Python via
rclpy — crashes in the same call, in the same libraries. The writer is not reached: the
process dies at the render warm-up loop that runs *before* any publisher is wired.

## Where that leaves the cause

Not the GPU, not the driver, not the image writer, not the ROS 2 bridge, not the
articulation, not the world, not the RTX lidar, not the IMU sensors, and not our camera
parameters — each of those is individually exonerated by a probe that survives five
render steps and returns a valid frame.

The fault needs something else in `run.py`'s process that probe E does not reproduce.
The strongest remaining candidate, from the crash dump itself, is the **rclpy executor
threads spinning concurrently with the SDG/OmniGraph render pipeline** — probe E has no
such threads, the twin has two, and the crash is inside OmniGraph's executor
(`libomni.graph.exec`). That is a hypothesis, **not** a result: it has not been tested and
must not be quoted as one.

## The next experiment, for whoever picks this up

Add rclpy spin threads to probe E and step. If it crashes, the cause is the concurrency
and the fix is to quiesce or serialise the executors across `world.step(render=True)`.
If it survives, bisect the remaining delta between probe E and `_setup_twin_ros` — the
TF/clock/odom OmniGraph setup is the next-largest untested block.

Probes A–E are reproducible from `/tmp/isaacrun/c23probe{,C,D,E}.py` patterns recorded in
this session's log; each is ~40 lines and runs in about two minutes inside the sim
container.

## Disposition

**Camera work is parked here**, per Mohammed's instruction, with the cause re-scoped from
"environment/GPU" to "an in-process interaction in Isaac 5.1's synthetic-data pipeline,
reproduced on 4080 and 4090, with five components individually cleared". Non-camera work
is unaffected: the montage's chase/fixed/offscreen tracks do not need this, only the PiP
camera track does.
