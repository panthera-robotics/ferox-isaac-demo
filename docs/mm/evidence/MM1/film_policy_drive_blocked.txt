film.py --drive policy — why the robot is thrown, measured rather than guessed
=============================================================================

The gain read-back added to _policy_drive reports, on every attempt:

    policy gains: kp[min,max]=(40.0,35809.9)  kd[min,max]=(0.000,5.000)

deploy.yaml's gains top out around 150 and no joint has zero damping. A kp of
35,810 alongside a kd of 0.000 is neither deploy.yaml's values nor the USD's
(625 / 0.0). G1VelocityPolicy.initialize() is therefore NOT applying the deployed
controller's gains when the policy is constructed inside film.py, and the robot has
never been driven by the controller the clip is supposed to show.

That is the root cause of every "flung robot" clip, and it explains why the
symptom survived each earlier fix.

Attempts, each ruling something out:
  1. paused timeline               -> 600 identical frames        FIXED (world.play)
  2. spawn at z=0.116, lying down  -> robot on the floor          FIXED (env.yaml pos)
  3. World at 1/60 s               -> 50 Hz control became 15 Hz  FIXED (1/200)
  4. two ArticulationViews on one prim                            FIXED, not the cause
  5. spawn passed post-hoc, default state still z=0               FIXED, not the cause
  6. initialize() before the world plays                          not the cause
  7. extra world.reset() after construction  -> base at z=0.046, gains UNCHANGED. Reverted.

Still open: make initialize() apply deploy.yaml's gains in a standalone scene, or
drive the clip from the live sim (run.py), where the identical policy and checkpoint
demonstrably walk -- MM1 measured N@0.2 at 2.4 % error there.

No clip has been shot. A montage of a robot being thrown, captioned as a walk, is
what the campaign's media rules exist to prevent.
