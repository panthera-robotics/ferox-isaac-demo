Why does this separation matter?

Because replacing the hand should not mean throwing away the walking policy or the learned manipulation model. The E2 campaign proved that the body policy transfers independently of the hand.

  • Trained with arm-forward/payload conditions.
  • Recovered lateral motion and moving turns under manipulation load.

6. Whiteboard

Historical donor success

What worked

The donor simulation physically wrote G1 / OK. The complete workflow produced seven strokes and thousands of pen-down contact samples. It was good enough for the hero presentation, although the stricter research metric still had path/coverage failures.

Current E2 issue

What broke

The old marker grip itself is incompatible with E2 geometry, so the writer never reaches a valid writing start. The E2 campaign therefore classified the old whiteboard grip as HAND_TRANSFER_FAIL — geometry.

7. GR00T: what really happened

Compatible G1 + Inspire route

Panthera integrated a GR00T N1.6 checkpoint built around a G1 + Inspire pick-and-place embodiment.

R1: normalization defect

The fine-tune kept the base checkpoint's normalization statistics. Many arm targets were clipped, so the model could not command enough reach. 0/10 contact.

R2: first learned enclosure

After fixing normalization, the model reached farther and produced the first held-out finger/thumb enclosure. The remaining data contained a long static pre-grasp dwell, which taught a memoryless policy to pause.

Action-chunk horizon diagnosis

The lift lived later in each predicted action chunk. When a longer prefix was executed, 3/3 donor diagnostic cells produced model-controlled lifts.

E2 transfer

Frozen weights transferred poorly at first, but the longer execution-horizon E2 row produced 4/10 model lifts. Final classification: SHORT_ADAPTATION_REQUIRED.

Next GR00T rule: do not restart from base N1.6. First calibrate the real RH56E2, rerun the frozen checkpoint, then do only the short embodiment adaptation that evidence says is needed.

8. SONIC

What SONIC is for

Whole-body humanoid motion: natural locomotion, reference tracking, VR/teleoperation and expressive movements.

  • standing
  • walking
  • dances
  • large turns
  • squats

Why ma2 in Isaac?

The SONIC reference body did not balance cleanly in the Panthera PhysX twin, while the in-house RL locomotion policy did. So ma2 is the current Isaac body controller; SONIC remains a separate MuJoCo/future-hardware path.

12. Test yourself

Which component currently controls manipulation-aware walking in Isaac?
ma2_7325. GR00T handles manipulation logic; SONIC is a separate whole-body motion route.
Did changing from donor hands to E2 destroy locomotion?
No. The body policy achieved DIRECT_TRANSFER_PASS.
Why does the old whiteboard grip fail?
Because the old grip depends on a donor thumb contact point the E2 distal thumb cannot physically reach.
Should GR00T restart from scratch?
No. Calibrate the installed E2, rerun the frozen checkpoint, then use a short embodiment adaptation if needed.
